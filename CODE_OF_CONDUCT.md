# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This project exists so people can find out what is wrong with their
codebase without being made to feel bad about it.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.
- "Vibe coded" is a description, not an insult. This toolkit is built *for* fast-moving
  codebases and for the people who ship them.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks in issues, pull requests or
  discussions.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **jaco@leongael.xyz**. Reports are handled privately.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock or remove contributions that violate this code, and may
block accounts for repeated or severe violations.
